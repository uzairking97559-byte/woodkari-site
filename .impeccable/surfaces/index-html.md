# Surface brief: woodkariexports.com (home, product pages, bulk page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: bulk buyers (hotels, gifting companies, shops, importers) and single-piece home buyers in India, mostly on phones. Action: start a WhatsApp chat (bulk or single order); email second. Proof on hand: real plain photos of the room tray and fluted box; labelled styled/design pictures; since 2008, four karigars, CNC router, laser, resin, wooden pieces finished by hand. Constraints: every claim OWNER or READ (PRODUCT.md "still undecided"); no cart, no UPI claim; resin trays say "made in Sambhal". Resin trays lead the page (owner: room tray and box are not the stars).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The owner pinned the look with his own reference picture (14 Nov, 23:03): a large-brand Indian decor shop in deep forest green, light elegant serif, centred wordmark, category tabs under the header, a picture-led hero with "Made in Sambhal." and one green action, a two-column product grid with green prices, and a green band for hotels, shops and gifting. Refuses the navy/gold and the walnut versions he rejected ("blue achha nahi lag raha", "AI jaisi lag rahi hai").
OWN-WORLD: White ground, greige stone #f2efe9 bands, forest green #1d4a3a as the brand field (hero panel, bulk band, buttons, prices), deep green #143629 footer, soft gold #d6b877 only on green, brass #8a6a2c for step numerals and ribbon marks. Instrument Serif for display and the wordmark, Hanken Grotesk for everything else. 3px radii, hairlines, picture tags on every non-real photo.
STORY: A resin tray fills the first screen under "Made in Sambhal."; the visitor explores six pieces with sizes and prices, meets the bulk offer in a full green band (25 pieces, 50% advance, making time), sees engraving from 50, the made-on-order designs, the workshop facts and the buyers' questions, and taps WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: header (menu, centred Woodkari / SAMBHAL, search), tab row (All pieces, Acacia wood, Resin trays, Bulk orders...), 4:5 slideshow with the headline, one line, "Explore the collection" and "Bulk orders" over the picture, progress bars at its foot. Desktop: 46/54 split, green panel with the headline at up to 96px left, slideshow right.
FORM: pinned by the owner's reference picture; seed: none (pinned) — "aisa colors lagaoge achhi aur royal website lagegi... options lagao, badiya animation lagao, badi badi website jaisi banao". Signature interaction: tapping a piece morphs its picture into the product page (cross-page view transition); motion grammar: masked headline rise, slow picture drift with crossfade, curtain wipe on section pictures, staggered rise on cards, serif fact ribbon, drawer and search sheets that slide.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

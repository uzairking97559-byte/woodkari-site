## Platform

## Users
Two audiences, both confirmed by the owner (14 Nov 2026: "Dono"):
- **Bulk buyers** (the main business): hotels and hospitality suppliers (room trays for guest rooms), gifting companies and company HR (staff and New Year gifts, a name or logo on 50+ pieces), decor brands and shops, and later importers abroad. They come from a mail, a WhatsApp message or a listing, look at the pieces on a phone, and want to know quickly: what it is, the size, the material, the bulk minimum, and how to start an order on WhatsApp or by mail.
- **Single-piece buyers** in India who want one tray or a pair for their home or a gift, and pay the site price.

Most visitors arrive on a phone.

## Product Purpose
Woodkari Exports is a small Sambhal (Uttar Pradesh) maker of wooden and resin trays and boxes. The site shows what we make, at what size and price, and turns a visitor into a WhatsApp chat or a mail to sales@woodkariexports.com. Success = bulk enquiries (25+ pieces) and some single orders.

## Positioning
A real workshop in Sambhal, making these pieces since 2008, where most work is bulk orders. Four karigars; CNC router, laser engraving, resin. Finished by hand (owner, 14 Nov: sanding and polish by hand). A name or logo can be laser-engraved on orders of 50 pieces or more of one design. Acacia pieces (room tray, fluted box) can be made in any size.

## Operating Context
- Orders start on WhatsApp (+91 63971 21223) or by mail (sales@woodkariexports.com). No cart, no online payment on the site.
- Bulk: from 25 pieces per design; 50% advance (owner); making time about 10-15 days per 100 pieces; delivery at the buyer's cost.
- Resin trays are made in Sambhal by market makers and a friend's workshop (owner, 14 Nov), not in our own workshop: say "made to order in India" (or "with other makers near our workshop"), never "our workshop", for them.
- Pictures: real plain photos exist for the room tray and fluted box only; every other picture is a design picture and must be labelled "Design picture, made to order".

## Product Principles
1. Every line is true and checkable; a guess never goes on the site.
2. Bulk first, single pieces welcome.
3. The phone is the main screen; WhatsApp is the main button.
4. Show the real piece, its size and its material before anything else.

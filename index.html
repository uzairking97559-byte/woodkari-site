How we work

Wood
Mango or acacia wood, kiln-dried (8-12% moisture).
Trays (MDF)
MDF core with a resin top; plain MDF trays also available.
Finish
Walnut-tone polish or natural; food-safe finish on request.
Lead time
Samples in about 7 days. About 15-20 days for 100 pieces.
Payment
50% advance, balance before dispatch.
Shipping
Courier or freight at the buyer's cost, quoted before dispatch.
Compliance
Kiln-dried wood. We do not claim any certification at this stage.

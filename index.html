    The workshop

    Woodkari is a workshop in India, at work since 2008. Four karigars cut the wood on a CNC router, engrave by laser and finish our wooden pieces by hand. Our resin trays are made with other makers near our workshop.

    Questions buyers ask

    Not here? Ask us on WhatsApp.

    What is the minimum for a bulk order?

    Trays and boxes: 25 pieces of one design. Small pieces such as coasters and tissue boxes are made in larger lots; ask us.

    Can you put our name or logo on the pieces?

    Yes, on the wooden pieces, by laser, on 50 pieces or more of one design: a hotel name, a company logo or a couple's names.

    Can you change the size?

    The acacia pieces (the Room Tray and the Fluted Box Tray) can be made in any size, on order.

    How long does an order take?

    Wooden pieces: about 10 to 15 days for 100. Resin pieces: we tell you the time when you ask.

    How does payment work?

    A bulk order starts with a 50% advance. For a single piece, we confirm how to pay on WhatsApp.

    Who pays for delivery?

    Delivery is at the buyer's cost. We tell you the amount before the order leaves.

    Can I buy just one piece?

    Yes. Every piece with a price can be ordered on its own. Tap "Order on WhatsApp" on the piece and tell us your city and PIN code; we confirm the delivery charge, the time and how to pay.

    Tell us what you need.

    The piece, the size and how many. We reply on WhatsApp.
